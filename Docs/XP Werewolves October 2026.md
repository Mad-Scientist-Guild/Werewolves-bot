# **General**

The XP community is terrorized by a variety of evil creatures and everyone must work together to defeat them and ensure the survival of the group. To eliminate these monsters, a suspicious character can be voted on every day. This person will be lynched. But, evil creatures have dark powers that can eliminate the innocent or manipulate them. Will the XP community succeed in expelling all dark creatures? Or will the evil reign?

You will gain a private channel with the Gamemasters named after your role, in this channel you can specify what you want to do, and how you want to do it. You can also converse with the Gamemasters privately about the rules of the game and/or your specific role.

A lot of actions can be automatically executed by the XP Werewolves Bot on Discord. To find out which ones and how to use them please refer to the second piece of documentation provided in the Discord server, or check your private channel to learn the commands of your role.

# **Round system**

In this online version of Werewolves we use the real days and nights as phases in the game. Every day, the day starts at 8am and lasts until 7 pm, with the nights lasting from 7:00 pm till 8 am in the morning. 

## **Day (8:00 – 19:00)**

During the day, the people with **(Day action)** can do their actions. People will gain their information from previous nights, and anyone that died will be moved to their new channels.   
Everyone can also vote to lynch one person using the bot, to do so please type /lynch vote @name in your private channel. You can change your vote at any time, up until the 19:00 deadline.

## **Newspaper (12:00)**

Everyday at 12:00 we release the newspaper of that day, this will contain information about the events that happened last day and night. In addition, from then on, the townsfolk can start voting on the Lynch.

## **Night (19:01 – 00:00)**

At the start of the night, the Lynch vote ends and either someone dies or the town abstains from killing.  
During the night, all participants with a special role can perform their **(Night action)** for their roles in their own chat channels. If they belong to a group, all people with this group have access to this group. Please do your actions before 0:00, otherwise your action might not happen.

## **Sleep time (00:01 \- 7:59)**

During this time you won’t be able to do your nightly actions anymore, these are already locked in. You can however still chat in village-chat and other channels you are a part of. (please get enough sleep.

# **First day**

On the first day, nothing will happen, you can talk to everyone in any channel you can access. Your role should be clear, and any questions can be asked about the game, questions about roles are better asked directly to the gamemaster(s) through your private channel. Some roles get a specific action on the first night, such as: Cupid and the Wild Boy which they will get the chance to. And, of course, the wolves choose their first victim (and anyone else with a nightly action can use it).

# **Vote**

Participants can vote during the day on who they want to lynch, this is not mandatory. The one with the most votes is killed, and then it is revealed what the lynched person’s faction was. 

If more than half of the players forget or choose to not vote, no one will be lynched.  
When there is a tiebreak in number of votes, the Mayor will have to decide who will be lynched (see: Mayor). 

Everyone's vote will be published for everyone to see after the voting is over. (see: Cunning wolf', for an exception).

# **I'm dead, now what?**

If your faction wins, you still win\! When you die in the game you will be added to the 'death channel' (See “Vengeful Spirit” for an exception) in this channel you can talk to other dead people. In this channel, anything can be shared, as long as it is not shared with the living players (outside of the contents of the game rules). 

The Spiritualist is able to talk to the dead. So stay ready to answer any questions they may have in order to push the game in your favor.

It is also possible for the Lich to turn you into a Skeleton or Witch to revive you again, please read those roles for more clarification.

# **Factions and roles**

In this game, there are a couple of factions (Villagers, Werewolves, Undead, Lovers, and the Coven); some roles gain their faction later or can switch during the game. Roles can be switched between nights/days.  
Win Conditions:

Villagers: You have removed the Werewolves, Coven & Undead factions.

Werewolves: You killed every other faction.

Undead: You win if there are more undead than alive players in the game.

Lovers: You stay alive with your lover (and Cupid).

Coven: You have removed everyone from your kill list.

# **Families**

Each family will consist of 6+- members and will gain their own private chat to be able to talk into, the roles of your other family members will be unknown to each other, and you may well be in the same household as an evil-aligned person. 

\- Families have nothing to do with the win condition, that is always related to your faction.

# **Evil roles**

These roles want to take over the village, there is more than one evil faction, and they are allowed to work together, but ultimately only one can win the game.

**Werewolves**  
The werewolves try to eat all the other villagers together. The werewolves have a joint chat and may discuss at night who they will eat, the werewolves can choose not to kill anyone for an evening. When deciding who to kill, most wolves have to agree with the target, if the wolves cannot reach a consensus no one gets eaten.  
Werewolves win when there are more werewolves than non-werewolves alive.  
The person they eat will die at the end of the night (unless that person is protected in some way). The following roles belong to the werewolves.

## **Ancient wolf (Werewolves) (Night action)** 

Once per game, the Ancient wolf can choose to not let the target of the werewolves be killed, but instead, this person will become part of the Werewolf faction, gain access to the werewolves private channel and help choose who to eat. This person keeps their original abilities.

- *If a turn fails, the Ancient wolf keeps their ability*  
- *If the Vampire Lord and the Ancient wolf try to turn the same person, one will automatically fail.*

## **\*Turned wolf (Werewolves)**

No player can start with this role, but it can be acquired.  
The Turned wolf joins the Werewolf faction but they keep access to any chat they previously had access to, they also keep their ability. They lose their alliance to their previous faction, and now win with the Werewolves.

- *A turned werewolf stays werewolf if they get revived*

## **Apprentice wolf (Werewolves) (Night action)**

The night after a fellow wolf dies the Apprentice wolf can choose to take up their role. They have to do this that night or the option is gone forever. Once they have changed they basically become the other wolf, losing their original apprentice ability.

- *The charges carry over from the other wolf, for example if the Ancient wolf has already used their ability the Apprentice wolf can not use it again.*  
- *If the wolf is revived by the Witch, and the Apprentice wolf has taken on their role, the revived wolf becomes a wolf without abilities.*

## **Bloodhound (Werewolves) (Night action)**

Once a night the Bloodhound can check up on one person, they will be informed if this person has left their house during the night. 

- *See the list of people leaving the house at the bottom for potential roles.*

## **Cultist (Werewolves)**

The Cultist is a villager that is part of the Werewolves faction. This person is in the werewolves chat, but is not allowed to vote on whom to eat. If they are the last person standing in the Werewolf faction they lose automatically. Due to them not actually being a werewolf, they will show up as villagers on the Seer & Fox, and won’t be killed by the Monster Hunter.

##  **Cunning wolf (Werewolves)  (Day action)** 

The Cunning wolf may name a second name when casting their vote (for the lynch), when the vote is announced this second name will be listed as a vote instead of the  
name the Cunning wolf actually voted for.

## **Feral wolf (Werewolves) (Night action)**

Thrice per game, the Feral wolf can, in addition to the normal wolf kill, individually choose another player to eat that night. 

- *This player can be of any faction, including werewolves.*

## 

## **Normal wolf (Werewolves)**

This wolf has no additional powers but gains some extra information, at least one (villager) role will not be in the game, and this role will be shared with the normal wolf. They can share this information with the other wolves or keep it to themselves.

## 

## **Rabid wolf (Werewolves) (Night action)**

Once per game, the Rabid wolf can choose to go on a killing spree. 

Steps of the killing spree:  
\- Choose another living player (by name)  
\- Guess their role

- *If the target was the Vigilante or the Monster Hunter (armed with silver), you die (in epic battle).*  
- *If the guess was correct, but that person is protected, nothing happens, your killing spree ends.*  
- *If the guess was correct, that person dies & you can pick another living player.*  
- *If the guess was wrong, nothing happens, your killing spree ends.*  
- *The Actor can be guessed as Actor regardless of what role they copy, but if they take on the Vigilante role you still die.*

## **Shadow wolf (Werewolves) (Night action)**

Once per night, the Shadow wolf may stalk a player. If the player leaves their house, they find the Shadow wolf waiting for them. The player is forced to retreat and can not perform their night action if they leave their house. The Shadow wolf can not use this ability on the same player twice, ever.

- *If the Shadow wolf happens to stalk the Monster Hunter when they’ve acquired silver, the Shadow wolf is killed when the Monster Hunter leaves their house.*  
- *If the Shadow wolf happens to stalk the Vigilante, the Shadow wolf is killed when the Vigilante leaves their house.*  
- *If the Actor takes on the Vigilante role, the above rule applies.*  
- *The targeted player is notified that the Shadow wolf has visited them last night, they will not lose their ability if it has limited uses.*

# **Undead**

The undead try to take over the town in the name of their master, any dead player can be resurrected and turned to the undead faction. The undead faction wins if there are more undead than alive players in the game.

The Lich, Vampire Lord & Vampire Spawn, share one chat.

## **Lich (Undead) (Night action)**

The Lich has to amass an army to take over the town of XP. Once a night the Lich can choose two different (dead) players and try to guess the role for both of them. Each person guessed correctly in this way gets resurrected as a skeleton. These skeletons join a private chat with the Lich. When the Lich dies, so do all their skeletons.

- *Lich can only die from Lynch.*

## **\*Skeleton (Undead) (public role)**

No player can start with this role, but it can be acquired.  
Once you become a Skeleton through the Lich, you once again access the village chat but can no longer access the death chat. You can communicate in the village chat using reactions\*. You will join a private chat with the Lich and all other skeletons. 

- ***\*Skeletons are not allowed to use anything but the default \+ Werewolves emotes and should refrain from using the letters to spell things when reacting to messages\!***  
- *Skeletons are allowed to relay the information they learned in the death chat.*  
- *Skeletons can not vote for lynching during the day, but they can be disposed of by being killed during the lynch or any other means.*   
- *Skeletons can't gain the Lover, Turned wolf, Changeling, or Vampire Spawn role. If a Skeleton dies they cannot be re-resurrected.*  
- *When the Lich dies, all skeletons also die.*  
- *Skeletons will be allowed to play tennis.*

## **Vampire Lord (Undead) (Night action)**

The Vampire Lord can choose one target to bite each night and kill them. If the Vampire Lord does not have a Vampire Spawn, they can specify that they want their bitten target to turn into a Vampire Spawn instead of dying. The Vampire Spawn will be added to the undead private channel the next morning (8:00), and the Vampire Spawn becomes part of the Undead faction. A bitten target can only become a Vampire Spawn if the Vampire Lord is still alive. 

- *If the Vampire Lord and the Ancient wolf try to turn the same person, one will automatically fail.*

## **\*Vampire Spawn (Undead)**

No player can start with this role, but it can be acquired.  
The Vampire Spawn joins the Undead faction but they keep access to any chat they previously had access to, they also keep their ability. They lose alliance to their previous faction and now win with the Undead.  
If the Vampire Lord dies, and the Vampire Spawn has participated in at least one kill the Vampire Spawn will turn into a Vampire lord. Once it does, it loses its previous ability.

- *If the Vampire Spawn hasn’t participated in a kill they will remain Spawn when the Vampire Lord dies.*  
- *If there are 2 vampire spawns, they themselves can decide together who becomes the new vampire lord when their old lord dies.*

# **Coven**

The Coven wants to win by performing their ritual to serve the Great Powers beyond the Veil. They long to hold the power over the town. The Coven wins by eliminating the threats certain people pose to their Coven. They will be given a list of 18 roles currently in the manual and will have to try and remove all of them from the game by any means necessary to win, 3 of these 18 roles will not be in the game.

The Coven is a group of powerful magic users that wish to take control of this town from the shadows, however they are unaware of each other's identities. During the night each Coven member can guess the identity of a single other member, if they guess correctly they will join forces from there on out.

- *When finding a fellow member the Coven members will gain access to a shared group chat with the members that they’ve met, they will not gain contact with any members they haven’t personally found.*  
- *The Kill list becomes available to a Coven member if they have at least found 1 other Coven member.*  
- *Roles on the kill list are the original roles, for example if the Grave Robber takes on another role, they still count as Grave Robber for the kill list, or when a player is added to the Brothers, they do not count as a Brother for the kill list.*  
- *There are always 4 Coven members at the start of the game.*

## **Curseweaver (Coven) (Night Action)**

The Curseweaver can curse someone during the night, they get to pick 1 of 2 options:

- *Curse of the Puppet:* This player’s nightly ability target will be switched to someone else at random.  
- *Curse of Transformation:* This player is turned into a lizard and can’t perform their nightly action. 

The Curseweaver can not target the same person twice in a row.  
The person affected will be notified that they had been cursed at the start of the next morning.

- *The Puppet curse’s random target is decided by the wheel consisting of all alive players, known Coven members are excluded.*  
- *Skeletons cannot be targeted by the Curse of the Puppet.*  
- *Wolves get their personal ability randomized or blocked, not their group wolf killing vote.*

## **Diviner (Coven) (Night Action)**

The Diviner can pick a house to divine. Picking from 1 of 3 options.

- *Detection:* They get to know one person that left the house that night.   
- *Scrying:* You can specify a time of 60 minutes to listen in on a house, this works like the Peeking girl ability. (Example 20:14 \- 21:14 or 21:00 \- 22:00)  
  The Diviner will receive the first 15 minutes of conversation time from the family chat (if there is any), from the moment a family member talks in the chat.

  The discord usernames will not be revealed but any names mentioned in the chat will be.

  The time picked must be in the future.

- *Vision:* They get to know 1 role that is living within a house.

- *If the Diviner uses Detection and no one leaves the house that night, they are notified that nothing happened.*  
- *Known Coven members known to them are excluded from being picked with Detection or Vision.*

## **Enchantress (Coven) (Day action)**

Once per day, the Enchantress can enchant another player. They know 3 charms from their spellbook that they can use, picking only 1 at a time:

- *Charm of the Open Book:* The enchanted player must answer a yes or no question asked by the Enchantress. The question can not directly be related to the player’s role, ability, or faction. The enchanted player must answer honestly.   
- *Charm of Protection:* The enchanted player is protected by magical nature. They cannot be killed during the coming night. The Enchantress can not use this charm twice on the same person, ever.  
- *Charm of Revenge:* If the enchanted player is killed during the night, the Enchantress is fueled with rage and can choose to unleash their magic upon one player, killing them. They can kill with their revenge the next day between 8:00 & 18:00. They then lose their ability to use their *Charm of Protection*. 

The Charm loses its effect the next morning. The player affected will be notified that they have been enchanted.

- *The Charm of Protection does not work against Turning.*

## **Hag Mother (Coven) (Night Action)**

The Hag Mother picks another person within the village during the night, this person is unbeknownst to themselves a Changeling. After 3 nights this person grows up to become a hag / original role hybrid, losing alliance to its previous faction.

Because of its motherly instincts, the Hag Mother can search for 3 people instead of 1 each night.

Upon the full coven finding each other, the Hag Mother gains the ability to guide her Coven by checking the Coven kill list to see if one of the roles is currently alive, or not.

- *If the Hag Mother dies before the Changeling becomes part of the Coven faction, the Changeling does not grow up to be a hybrid, and does not become part of the Coven faction.*

## **\*Changeling (Coven)**

No player can knowingly start with this role, but it can be acquired.

After 3 nights the Changeling grows up to become a hag / original role hybrid.  
After they’ve become a hybrid, the Changeling joins the Coven faction but they keep access to any chat they previously had access to, they also keep their ability. They lose alliance to their previous faction and now win with the Coven.

# **Villagers**

All villagers (Including dead ones) win when all other factions are eliminated (Werewolves, Coven & Undead).

## **Actor (Villagers) (Day action)**

Once per day, the Actor can take on another villager role. For that day and night only, they receive that role and can use that power (renewed if the power was limited). The Actor can not take on a villager role twice, ever.

- *The Actor cannot take on the role of the Adopted Child, a Brother, the Grave Robber or Cupid.*  
- *If you copy the Spiritualist, they don’t join the chat with the Vengeful Spirit. They can only ask 1 question to the dead.*  
- *If you copy the Monster Hunter, you do not have silver.*

## **Adopted Child (Villagers)**

The Adopted Child has access to one additional family channel without being publicly known as being part of that other family. This extra family will be notified of who the Adopted Child is.

- The Adopted Child can join their second family at any time during the game.

## **The Brother (x3) (Villagers) (Any time)**

The bond of Brothers can transcend family (and gender) and doesn't have to be related to blood. All Brothers will share a private channel, which means they know each other’s role and can talk tactics. At any time the Brothers can unanimously choose to add another to their chat (this person’s role will not be revealed).

## **Cupid (Lovers / Villagers) (Night action)**

Cupid may link two people on the first night. These two are now linked in the fight against anyone who keeps them apart. Cupid is protected by the power of this bond and can not die from lynching while both lovers are alive. Cupid (and the lovers) wins if the lovers (and potentially Cupid) are alive by the end of the game.  
If Cupid is lynched by the villagers, their role will be revealed, but they don’t die. If one, or more of the lovers die, Cupid joins the Villagers faction. 

## **Fox (Villagers) (Night action)**

Once per night the Fox can point to 3 people, if one of these is not from the Villager faction the Fox will be notified. If all of them are villagers the Fox will be informed and will lose their ability for future nights.

## **Gambler (Villagers) (Night action)**The Wise Monkey

Once per night, the Gambler can visit a player. They get to know their full role, but if they visit one of the evil factions (Werewolves, Undead or Coven) they die tragically.

- *If visiting the Lich, they die and immediately become a skeleton.*

## **Grave Robber (Villagers, Any Faction) (Any time)**

The Grave Robber can plunder the grave of one of the dead once. This person will then be told what role that person had and will take over that person's role. They then gain the powers of that role (renewed if the power was limited) and from that moment belong to the group to which that role belonged. 

- *Plundering during the day grants the role at the end of Daytime (19:00)*  
  - *The Grave Robber can use their Night action if the role has one*  
- *Plundering during the night grants the role at the end of Nighttime (8:00)*  
  - *The Grave Robber can use their Day action if the role has one*  
- *The Grave Robber can't plunder the graves of Skeletons.*

## **Insomniac (Villagers) (Day action)**

The Insomniac has the choice, during the day, either: to be notified of how many family members left the house during the coming night or to be notified if and how many people have visited the family that night because they were unable to sleep.

- *Only one wolf will be heard visiting by the Insomniac.*  
- *If the Slut visits the Insomniac they learn each other's roles.*

- *See the list of people leaving the house at the bottom for potential roles.*

## **Locksmith (Villagers) (Night action)**

Once per night, the Locksmith can put a lock on someone's house. While there is a lock on your house during the night, people cannot enter your house (Villagers, Werewolves,  the Undead, or the Coven), and the person locked up can not use their powers if they leave their house to do so during the night. As a Locksmith you can not use this power twice on the same person, ever.

## 

## **Monster Hunter (Villagers) (Night action)**

The Monster Hunter needs to find the Silver Angel. Once per night they can point to one player and check if they are the Silver Angel. 

Every night after the Silver Angel has been found: The Monster Hunter can point to one player and will kill them if this player is from any of the evil factions. 

- *If the Monster Hunter has acquired silver and they are stalked by the Shadow wolf, they kill the Shadow wolf and proceed to also kill their original target if they’re from an evil faction.*

## **Old Hunter (Villagers) (Night action)**

Once per night, the Old Hunter can choose to coat their sword in wolfsbane. Their stash is limited to 3 uses. If the Old Hunter is visited by a werewolf and their sword is coated, the werewolf suffers from the wolfsbane and will die the next morning, the Old Hunter lives. If the Old Hunter is visited by the wolves when their sword isn’t coated, the wolf survives and the Old Hunter dies.

- *The Old Hunter can only kill werewolves.*  
- *When a wolf is killed this way, this is announced in the newspaper the morning after.*  
- *The werewolf is randomly picked from those attacking.*

## **Pacifist (Villagers) (Day action)**

Once per game, the Pacifist can force the whole vote to stop, this means no one will be lynched. The Pacifist gets to know the faction of the person who was about to be lynched.

- *The Pacifist can not stop the Ruthless Counselor’s 2nd Lynch.*

## **Peeking Girl (Villagers) (Any time)**

Once per day (midnight to midnight), the Peeking Girl can specify a 60-minute time frame when they want to listen in on the wolves. (Example 20:14 \- 21:14 or 21:00 \- 22:00)  
The Peeking Girl will receive the first 15 minutes of conversation time from the werewolf chat (if there is any), from the moment a wolf talks in the chat.

- *The discord usernames will not be revealed but any names mentioned in the chat will be.*  
- *The time picked must be in the future.*

## **Priest (Villagers) (Day action)**

Twice per game the Priest can bless a family. All the people in this family can’t die during this night. With the exception if a lover dies of heartbreak.

## **Prophet (Villagers) (Night action)**

Every night the Prophet can pray for a role. If the role is in play, the Prophet is given a list of 1/3 of the currently alive players, at least one of which has the specified role. If there is nobody with that role, the Prophet is alerted of that fact.

## **Ruthless Counselor (Villagers) (Day action)**

Twice per game, the Ruthless Counselor can call for a second lynch vote immediately after the first lynch, without the village having time to discuss. This means **all** discussion/faction channels will go on Lockdown (except the private role channels) until the second lynch results are posted. The Ruthless Counselor can not use this power 2 days in a row

- *The 2nd lynch will be announced at 20:00. You have until then to vote.*  
- *During this lynch Abstaining is not an option.*  
- *Someone will die in the second lynch.*  
- *When there’s a tie, the Ruthless Counselor gets to decide who dies.*  
- *The Pacifist can not stop this lynch.*

## **Seer (Villagers) (Night action)**

The Seer can choose one person each night and they will then be told this person's faction (Werewolves, Undead, Coven, Lovers or Villagers) If the Seer designates a person that the Seer has previously designated, the Seer will be told the specific role of the person.

*Special interaction:*

- *If the Seer checks the faction of the Grave Robber before they robbed a grave the Seer will learn their role since they haven’t joined a faction yet.*  
- *The Cultist will show up as a Villager, if checked again they will show up as the Cultist.*  
- *The Changeling will show up as their original faction. If the Changeling has become a hybrid, they then show up as Coven. If the Seer checks the Changeling again, they will show up as their original role.*

## **Silver Angel (Villagers) (Night action)**

The Silver Angel is allowed to protect one person every night (including themselves). That person cannot die this night from the Werewolves, Coven or Undead. The Silver Angel cannot protect the same person twice in a row.

- *Protection works against turning.*

*Note for the Silver Angel: Read the “Monster hunter”*

## **Spiritualist (Villagers) (Any time)**

The Spiritualist can communicate with the dead and can ask them a Yes or No question once a day. The Spiritualist will receive an answer to this when all the dead have responded, or at the end of Daytime (19:00)/Nighttime (00:00) whichever comes first. The Spiritualist will not learn exactly which dead gave which answer, but they will learn the number of yes answers and no answers. The dead are not allowed to lie on this question.

- *The Spiritualist can also communicate with the Vengeful Spirit via a discord chat.*

## **Telepath (Villagers) (Any time)**

Once per day (8:00-00:00), the Telepath can send an anonymous message to another player (who is still alive). This other player will receive this exact message from a Game Master (anonymously) and is able to respond to it. The message limit (both ways) is 20 words.

## **Trapmaker (Villagers) (Night action)**

# **Additional roles**

These rolls are available on top of your regular roll, when acquired they can change the loyalty of the receiving player. It is possible to have multiple additional roles.

## **Mayor (doesn’t change faction) (public role)**

The Mayor is elected by ballot on the first day. If the votes for a lynch are tied, the Mayor's vote will be decisive.

As soon as a player becomes Mayor, they must indicate who they want to replace them if they die. They may change this choice at any time before their death. If the new Mayor dies immediately, they will still have the chance to appoint a replacement.

## **The Vengeful Spirit (doesn’t change faction)**

Can you imagine dying first? That's just bad\! Because of all that anger and dishonesty, you gain some power... The first player to die will turn into a Vengeful Spirit. The Vengeful Spirit can communicate fully with the Spiritualist via a private channel, but not with other dead players. In addition, the Vengeful Spirit has three nights to figure out who, or what killed them. If they do, they can then avenge themself by killing them. The Vengeful Spirit can only guess once in the three nights. If they guess wrong, no one dies and they still go on to the afterlife.

*Special interactions:*  
*\-If the Vengeful Spirit was killed by the werewolves collectively the Vengeful Spirit can pick & kill any of the werewolves.*  
*\-If the Vengeful Spirit was killed by the Feral wolf’s action, they can only pick & kill the Feral wolf.*  
*\- If the Vengeful Spirit was a lover, whose partner was turned. They need to kill the Ancient wolf/Vampire Lord, depending on which turned their partner.*
